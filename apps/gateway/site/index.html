Election Integrity Monitor

Could documented government actions plausibly change control of the U.S. House?

An evidence-backed assessment of the risk that House control could be determined by improper use of governmental process rather than by voters — not a forecast. Federal rules, voting-rights litigation, state legislation, and court rulings are ingested daily and scored by a published deterministic model: vulnerability, suppressed by institutional resistance, weighted by how pivotal each race is to control.

Sources: Federal Register · CourtListener · SCOTUS opinions · OpenStates · FEC · Census · MIT Election Lab · GDELT
